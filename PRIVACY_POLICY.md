# Privacy Policy for VitaTrack

**Effective Date:** June 17, 2026

VitaTrack ("we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how your information is handled when you use the VitaTrack mobile application.

## 1. Data Collection and Storage
VitaTrack is designed to be a fully local application. 
- **Local Database**: All health data, habits, medications, and task history are stored exclusively in a local SQLite database on your device.
- **Photos**: Any photos you take or attach (e.g., for medication identification) are stored locally in the app's internal storage on your device.

## 2. Data Sharing
We **do not** share, sell, or trade your data with any third parties. Since there is no backend server or cloud synchronization, your data never leaves your device.

## 3. Telemetry and Tracking
VitaTrack **does not** collect any telemetry, analytics, or usage data. We do not use any tracking cookies or third-party monitoring tools.

## 4. Permissions
The app may request the following permissions:
- **Camera/Gallery**: Only to allow you to attach reference photos to your medications or tasks. These images are kept local to the app.

## 5. Third-Party Services
VitaTrack does not integrate with any third-party services that collect your data.

## 6. Children's Privacy
Because we do not collect any data, our application does not knowingly or unknowingly collect personal information from children.

## 7. Changes to This Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by the new "Effective Date" at the top of this document.

## 8. Contact Us
If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer via the official GitHub repository.
